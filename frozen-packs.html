Foolproof Kitchen Steps

How to Cook Like a Dumpling Master

Do not thaw! For best texture, cook directly from frozen using one of our three proven kitchen methods.

1. Authentic Steam

8 – 10 Minutes

Line bamboo steamer with parchment paper or cabbage leaves. Arrange dumplings 1 inch apart. Place over rapidly boiling water and steam covered for 8–10 minutes until plump and glossy.

2. Crispy Potsticker Fry

6 – 8 Minutes

Heat 1 tbsp oil in a non-stick pan over medium heat. Place frozen dumplings flat side down and sear 2 mins until golden. Add 1/4 cup water, cover tightly, and steam 5 mins until liquid evaporates.

3. Gentle Simmer Boil

6 Minutes

Bring a large pot of water or seasoned broth to rolling boil. Drop frozen dumplings in very beatiful and stir gently. Once they float to surface, simmer on medium for 3 more minutes. Serve with chili oil.
