By Train & Tram

Parliament & Flinders St Stations are a short 6-minute walk. Trams 86, 96, 11 & 12 stop right outside on Bourke & Swanston Streets.

Parking & Access

Chinatown Secure Parking (222 Russell St) and MidCity Arcade Car Park are both within a 150m walking distance from our door.
