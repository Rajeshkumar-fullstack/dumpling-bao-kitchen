Heritage & Evolution

Our Journey, One Dumpling at a Time

Our story begins with a simple idea: make delicious dumplings and bao that bring people closer together. Inspired by Asian comfort-food traditions, we focus on thoughtful preparation, balanced flavours, and food that feels welcoming. From choosing ingredients to presenting every plate, we aim to make every visit enjoyable.

Our Kitchen Creed

Simple Ingredients.
Thoughtful Preparation.
Memorable Flavours.

We focus on balanced flavours, enjoyable textures, careful preparation, and food that encourages sharing. Rather than overcomplicating tradition, our kitchen focuses on clean textures, aromatic broths, and tender doughs that let natural flavours shine.

From our crispy golden lace potstickers to pillowy steamed bao, every bite is crafted to encourage gathering, conversation, and the timeless pleasure of sharing good food with family and friends.
